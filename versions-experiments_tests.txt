# 2. Selection of V2, V11 and V17

After V25, rather than continuing to blindly optimize every RTL version, the three strongest candidates were compared physically.

### Candidate selection

**V2**

* Lowest power.
* Good physical implementation.
* Retained as the **low-power reference**.

**V17**

* Lowest synthesis cell count.
* Lowest recorded route wirelength and vias.
* Retained as the **synthesis/routing reference**.
* However, timing and power were worse.

**V11**

* Best overall balance of timing, power, area/utilization and physical implementation.
* Clean DRC/LVS/Antenna.
* Clean hold timing.
* Therefore selected as the **final RTL architecture**.

> **Decision: V11 was frozen as the final 5-stage RTL implementation.**

# 5. Final Optimization Decision

For the GitHub repository, I recommend stating the conclusion like this:

 ### Final Optimization Decision

> The ALU64_CP design was evaluated through 25 RTL optimization versions, followed by multiple physical implementation experiments on the strongest V11 candidate.

> V17 achieved the lowest synthesized cell count and lowest recorded routing wirelength, while V2 achieved the lowest power. However, V11 provided the best overall balance between synthesis size, routing, power, setup timing, hold timing, and physical signoff results.

> Therefore, **V11 was frozen as the final 5-stage architecture**. Additional experiments with timing-driven placement and post-global-routing optimization produced limited timing improvements and, in some cases, introduced hold violations or worsened total negative slack.

> The final V11 implementation successfully completed the RTL-to-GDSII physical flow with **DRC, LVS, antenna, power-grid and hold checks passing**. The remaining limitation is that the **10 ns setup timing target was not closed at the worst SS corner**. Further architectural exploration, particularly the 6-stage alternative, was intentionally stopped because its increased sequential hardware and routing congestion did not provide sufficient overall benefit.
